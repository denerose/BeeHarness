**What happened:** Meta-review of bee-hindsight, run for real over this session. Concrete gaps found while executing it: (1) the Inputs list is loop-centric — phase list, plan, records — and a planner session has none; the runner improvises (session log, ADRs, status.md, conversation). (2) The Role field vocabulary (orchestrator/loop/reviewer/implementer/planner) doesn't cover a keeper-requested hindsight by a de-roled agent, and "any role when the keeper asks" isn't reflected in the template. (3) The examination questions are all deficit-focused — there is no channel for "what worked, keep doing it," so positive findings (subagent comprehension tests, the cold concurrency review, keeper-gated relays) have nowhere to land. (4) The suggestion-format template's slug (`YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>.md`) has drifted from ADR-007's role-initials convention (`…-pp-…`). (5) The skill doesn't say to list new suggestions in `.hivemind/status.md` awaiting the keeper — I had to remember.
**Suggestion:** one fix pass on `skills/bee-hindsight/` and its `assets/suggestion-format.md`: generalize the Inputs section to "re-read whatever state your role produced" with loop and planner examples; add "any role (keeper-requested)" to the role vocabulary; add a sixth examination question — "what worked that we should keep doing?" — with keep-notes allowed in the summary (not necessarily files); update the slug format to `YYYY-MM-DD-<role>-<slug>.md` per ADR-007; add a final step: "list new suggestions in status.md under Awaiting keeper." Related principle worth recording in the writing standard: design rituals from every party's seat — the wake-prompt step only existed once the keeper asked, because the handoff ritual was designed from the writer's side alone.
