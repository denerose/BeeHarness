# Suggestion — plain relations in prose: who does what to which file

**Date:** 2026-11-07
**Project:** BeeHarness
**Role:** power-planner (hindsight #2, keeper-challenged routing)
**Scope:** repo (docs/WRITING-STANDARD.md; affects every role's summaries, relays, reports)

## What happened

Recurring keeper corrections across the arc: motion-metaphor verbs
(ride, travel, carry, land, ship, feed) standing for plain
relations between artifacts — "the clause lands in AGENTS", "records
ride the branch". The metaphors erase the actor and the mechanism:
changes read like weather; nobody edits, approves, or commits. In a
harness built on provenance, that is anti-provenance language.

## Suggestion

One line in `docs/WRITING-STANDARD.md`:

> State relations plainly: who does what to which file. Motion
> metaphors (rides, lands, ships, travels) are banned in prose
> about artifacts — they delete exactly the actor and action the
> record exists to keep.

Seed: ME-003 + ME-PP-001. Applies to every keeper-facing surface:
summaries, relays, report text, cards.
