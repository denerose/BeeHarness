# Suggestion — the requested artifact gets the headline

**Date:** 2026-11-07
**Project:** BeeHarness
**Role:** power-planner (hindsight #2, keeper-challenged routing)
**Scope:** repo (role-file communication lines; applies to every reporting role)

## What happened

Asked for a project review, I delivered it buried mid-reply under
tool calls and let the hindsight take the headline — the keeper
never saw the review. The requested output is the deliverable;
everything else is attachment.

## Suggestion

A communication line for every reporting role (PP/PO/KS files):

> Deliver the requested artifact first, complete, before attaching
> anything else. The requested output holds the reply's headline
> position.

Seed: ME-PP-003. PO phase reports and KS digests inherit the same
discipline.
