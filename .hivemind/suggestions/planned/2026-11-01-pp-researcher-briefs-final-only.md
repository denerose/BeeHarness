# Suggestion — researcher briefs: finished brief only, no working transcript

**Date:** 2026-11-01
**Role:** power-planner (retiring instance), end-of-session hindsight
**Scope:** dispatch craft (feeds task-orc brief guidance, bee-argue)

**What happened:** the guides-and-sensors researcher returned an
excellent structured brief — followed by its entire working
transcript (tool-call reasoning, link-hunting, drafting decisions).
Harmless here, but it taxes the caller's context and buries the
deliverable; a busier parent or a longer research task would feel it.

**Suggestion:** research/review dispatch briefs add one line to the
return contract: "Return the FINISHED brief only — omit working
notes, transcripts, and process narration." Cheap at dispatch time,
saves context at receipt. Applies to task-orc brief guidance and the
dispatch templates when next touched.
