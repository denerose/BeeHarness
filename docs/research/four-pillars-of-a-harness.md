# The Four Pillars of a Harness — research reference

**Source:** "The Four Pillars of a Harness" (article written for
human audiences; drawing on OpenAI's framework) — keeper-provided
excerpt, 2026-11-01.
**Class:** lens — guides design judgment; never law. Law lives in
ADRs and `docs/`; nothing here overrides it.
**Gist:** four pillars — context engineering (right information at
the right time), architectural constraints (enforced, not
suggested), verification systems (separate evaluators; the
maker/checker split), feedback loops (fix the system, not the
output).

`.hivemind/designs/2026-11-01-pp-four-pillars-review.md` (our
architecture against the pillars; the pillar-2 gap became the
