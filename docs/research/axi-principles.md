# AXI principles (axi.md) — research reference

**Source:** axi.md — the ten agent-first CLI principles
(keeper-provided).
**Class:** law source — provenance only. The content is transcribed
into law at `docs/AXI.md` (ADR-014), which binds the `bee` CLI;

**Gist:** ten principles for agent-first CLI surfaces — token-lean
output, minimal schemas, truncation with `--full`, aggregates,
explicit empty states, structured errors/exit codes/idempotency/no
prompts/fail-loud, ambient-context-first, content-first bare call,
contextual disclosure, consistent help.
**Applied in:** `docs/AXI.md` (the law itself); ADR-014; every
`bee` subcommand design (P4 onward).
